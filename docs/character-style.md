## Art direction

- Stylized polished 3D hero-shooter aesthetic, inspired by Overwatch's readable shapes and simplified surfaces, with original character designs and a darker tone.
- Balanced athletic adult anatomy: believable torso-to-leg ratio, natural shoulders and pelvis, proportionate hands and shoes. Approximately 7.5 head heights is a guide, not a rigid measurement. Avoid elongated legs, undersized torsos, giant hands or oversized footwear.
- Modern futuristic techwear: fitted hoodies, layered technical fabrics, restrained harnesses and utility pouches, cargo trousers and sneakers or boots. Compact hoods, natural shoulder width, moderate sleeves and hems around the upper hip.
- Predominantly charcoal, black and cool dark gray. Separate materials through value, roughness and clean folds. Orange/amber is restricted to tiny accents such as mask lights; no broad orange panels, linings or trim by default.
- Masks are part of the current visual language. Vary individual faces, hair, silhouettes and equipment while preserving the shared style.
- Bare hands; no gloves, armored wrist guards or knee pads by default. Knees should read as continuous trouser fabric with folds.
- Clear silhouettes, sculpted cloth folds and controlled surface detail. Avoid photorealistic noise, blocky primitive shapes and chibi proportions.

## Meshy reference composition

One complete character, head to toe, front-facing relaxed A-pose, arms and bare hands separated from the torso and thighs. Empty hands unless a weapon is explicitly requested. Neutral light gray backdrop, even studio lighting, minimal perspective distortion, no labels, no graphic overlays and no extra views in the primary image.
